## BudgetBoard
# created by - Ryan Nave

This app is intended to track your monthly savings. By inputting your monthly income
and how much you want to save per month, BudgetBoard will calculate your leftover money per month.

BudgetBoard can create categories where you can allocate money in. This way, you can allocate money for different categories, and BudgetBoard will tell you if you are over budget.

## Stack

This app was made with Next.JS, TypeScript and Tailwind for the front end, with SupaBase and Clerk
for authentication and databases.
